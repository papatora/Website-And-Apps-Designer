# References & tools

A short, curated list. Everything here is something you'll actually open.

## Anti-slop research (read these first)
- [Anthropic `frontend-design` skill](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md): the five clusters generated design falls into, and a plan → review → build process
- [Improving frontend design through Skills](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills): Anthropic's fonts-to-avoid list, colour and motion guidance
- [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) by Paul Bakaus: craft floor, the "Refuse" list, visitor modes ([impeccable.style](https://impeccable.style))
- [Hallmark](https://github.com/Nutlope/hallmark) by Together AI: 58 slop-test gates ([usehallmark.com](https://www.usehallmark.com))
- What we took from each, and where they disagree: [research.md](research.md)

## Real-site design guides
- [Fudge DESIGN.md](https://github.com/scroobius-pip/fudge-design-md) (MIT): 1,160 guides reverse-engineered from real websites, covering palette, type scale, layout, components, and what to avoid. Search it by subject with `node tools/find-reference.mjs "<subject>"`. Take the DNA, not the hexes, and check font licences.
