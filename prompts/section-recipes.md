# Section recipes (alternatives to the defaults)

When you reach for the default section, pick one of these instead.

| Instead of… | Try |
|---|---|
| Centered hero with gradient blob | Asymmetric hero: headline left, **real product UI** (HTML mock) right. See `templates/landing`. |
| 3 icon feature cards | **Numbered steps** with a rule between each; or one feature shown in depth with a screenshot and a caption. |
| Logo wall of greyscale SVGs | A **sentence with a number** plus named customers in type: “Keeping the books for 1,240 studios, including …” |
| Carousel of 5-star testimonials | **One quote** with a specific result, set large, next to the metric it proves. |
| “Why choose us” checklist | A **before/after table** of the actual task. |
| 3-tier pricing with a glowing middle card | **Two plans** with honest limits, and who each is for. |
| FAQ accordion with fluff | `<details>` answering the real objections: data, lock-in, migration, cancelling. |
| Footer with 5 columns of links | One line: legal name, city, and the 4 links people actually use. |
| Card grid of portfolio work | An **index list** (year · client · discipline) with a hover reveal. See `templates/portfolio`. |
| Dashboard with 8 KPI cards | One **KPI strip** with 3–4 numbers, each with a comparison, then the table people actually work in. |
