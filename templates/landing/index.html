How it works

Three steps, and two of them happen without you.

Most studios spend 6–9 hours a month chasing receipts. Our customers report a median of 47 minutes after their second month.

  1. Connect your bank and inbox

    Read-only access through your bank's open-banking API and a Gmail or Outlook label you choose. We never see anything outside that label.

  2. Ledgerline matches overnight

    Receipts are paired with transactions by amount, date and merchant. Anything uncertain waits for you instead of being guessed.

  3. Review the leftovers, then export

    Usually three or four items. Fix them, press export, and your accountant gets a categorised ledger with every receipt attached.

Questions

Things people ask before signing up

Does Ledgerline replace my accountant?

No. It replaces the part of their invoice that's spent chasing you. They still do your filings; they just start from clean data.

Which banks are supported?

Any bank on the EU/UK open-banking networks — around 2,400 institutions. If yours isn't, you can upload a CSV or MT940 statement instead.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You get a full export (ledger plus every receipt as a PDF) and we delete the rest 30 days later. Nothing is kept for training or resale.

Can I try it on last month's books?

Yes. The trial imports up to 90 days of history, so you can compare against a month you've already closed.
