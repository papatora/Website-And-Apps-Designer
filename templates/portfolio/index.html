In focus · Kunsthal Zuid

A typeface that gets wider as the building gets taller.

The museum's three wings are different heights. The display face has a width axis mapped to each wing, so signage on the tallest floor sets in the widest cut. Visitors don't notice. Staff did, and started using it for their own posters.
